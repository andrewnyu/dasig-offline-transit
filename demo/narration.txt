Meet DASIG, an offline, voice-first jeepney copilot for Bacolod. The app ships with a verified twenty-four-route pack, so its core journey planner does not need a server. Airplane mode is already on. I type U S L S, and local fuzzy matching resolves it to La Salle without a cloud lookup. The destination is SM City Bacolod. Tap Plan entirely offline. DASIG samples route geometry into virtual stops and runs a round-based search inspired by RAPTOR, including walking access and transfer penalties. In seconds, it draws the route and returns the ride, boarding landmark, direction, fare, travel time, and walking estimate. The downloaded MapLibre region remains visible offline. Read Aloud uses an installed offline Android voice. Speech input, landmark matching, route calculation, map display, and spoken instructions can all stay on this device. DASIG does not cache a past answer. It computes a new trip while the cloud is gone.
